Table

Fish, in one screen

Six players, two teams of three, alternating seats. The deck is 54 cards split into nine half-suits of six: low (2–7) and high (9–A) of each suit, plus the eights and two jokers.

  • On your turn, ask one opponent for one specific card. You must already hold a card of that half-suit, and you may not ask for a card you hold.
  • They must hand it over if they have it, and you go again. If they don't, the turn passes to them.
  • When your team holds all six of a half-suit, claim it by naming exactly who holds each card. Get it wrong and the other team takes it — or, if the set was split across teams, nobody scores.
  • Out of cards? A teammate passes you the turn.

Nobody may bluff or misremember: every answer is truthful and every card is public once it moves. Everything the engine knows, you could know too.
