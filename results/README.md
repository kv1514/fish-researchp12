## What each producer is for

- **`scripts4/analytics4.py`** --- Strategy analytics for v0.4, on the same measures v0.3 reported.
- **`scripts4/learn_ask_objective.py`** --- Learn the v0.4 ask objective from CRN paired rollouts, then test it by play.
- **`tests4/test_learn_run_isolation.py`** --- A second learning run must not land on the first one's files.
- **`fish4/learn/rollout.py`** --- Common-random-numbers paired rollout evaluation of candidate asks.
- **`scripts4/ask_regret.py`** --- How much value does the ask objective leave on the table?
- **`scripts4/continuation_length.py`** --- How long does each continuation take to finish a deal?
- **`scripts4/decision_cost_profile.py`** --- Why is a decision 3.5 ms of fixed work plus 5.8 us per draw?
- **`scripts4/exact_or_feasibility.py`** --- Could the disjunctive constraints be handled exactly instead of sampled?
- **`scripts4/normaliser_variation.py`** --- How much does the choice model's dropped denominator actually vary?
- **`scripts4/precision_cost.py`** --- What does posterior precision cost, in milliseconds per decision?
- **`scripts4/rollout_target.py`** --- Does a marginal card still matter by the end of the game?
- **`scripts4/slope_by_resolution.py`** --- Does the rollout target's slope depend on how far into the deal we are?
- **`tests4/test_ask_regret.py`** --- The regret estimator, checked on data whose truth is known.
- **`scripts4/at_ask_verdict.py`** --- The pre-registered verdict on at-ask-time depth with an exact normaliser.
- **`scripts4/check_paper_numbers.py`** --- Does every number the paper quotes still match the results file it came from?
- **`scripts/find_champion.py`** --- Search for a new champion policy from the terms that individually won.
- **`fish4/oppmodel.py`** --- Inferring hidden cards from what players CHOSE to ask, not just legality.
- **`scripts4/choice_curve.py`** --- Measure the choice model instead of assuming it.
- **`scripts4/choice_form.py`** --- Is the power law the right shape, or only a shape that fits?
- **`tests4/test_choice_fit.py`** --- The conditional-logit fit, checked against data it cannot cheat on.
- **`tests4/test_choice_invariant.py`** --- You were dealt a card of every half-suit you have ever asked in.
- **`tests4/test_gamma_schedule.py`** --- Weighting an ask by where in the game it happened.
- **`scripts4/claim_criterion.py`** --- Does the voluntary claim threshold gate the right quantity?
- **`scripts4/continuation_compare.py`** --- Is the rollout target's slope about the continuation, or about the positions?
- **`scripts4/duel_depth_base_rate.py`** --- How often is the engine actually in a duel, and does gating the penalty matter?
- **`tests4/test_adaptive.py`** --- Adaptive style: the duel penalty and the score-aware tie-breakers.
- **`scripts4/ess_probe.py`** --- How much of the sampler's nominal precision actually survives reweighting?
- **`tests4/test_exact2.py`** --- Tests for the v2 exact solver: ground truth has to be genuinely true.
- **`fish4/exact2_study.py`** --- Validation and measurement harness for ``fish4.exact2``.
- **`tests4/test_negative_claims.py`** --- The refuted impossibility claim must not come back.
- **`scripts4/exact_bench4.py`** --- Absolute strength: how often does a policy choose a provably optimal move?
- **`fish4/exact2_corpus.py`** --- Harvest ``results/exact_positions_v2.json``: the v0.4 absolute benchmark.
- **`scripts/head2head.py`** --- Head-to-head between two champion candidates.
- **`scripts4/heterogeneity_across_runs.py`** --- Is between-block disagreement a property of the harness, or of one effect?
- **`scripts4/fit_hsvalue.py`** --- Fit the half-suit value model, then report how good it actually is.
- **`scripts4/infer_frontier.py`** --- Accuracy-versus-compute study of the fish4 inference backends.
- **`fish4/infer/base.py`** --- One interface for every hidden-state inference backend.
- **`scripts4/recheck_mdes.py`** --- Does using each cell's own noise change any verdict already reported?
- **`scripts4/pair_sd_model.py`** --- How big is a duplicate-deal pair's standard deviation, and what sets it?
- **`fish4/cli.py`** --- FishBot v0.4 command line: play it, analyse with it, simulate with it.
- **`fish4/perpetual.py`** --- Perpetual states in Literature: when can the game stop making progress?
- **`scripts4/perpetual_study.py`** --- Does Literature have a stalemate? Measuring dead positions and repetition.
- **`scripts4/posterior_accuracy.py`** --- How good is the posterior, measured against the truth it is guessing at?
- **`api/_engine.py`** --- Stateless game sessions for a serverless deployment.
- **`fish4/registry4.py`** --- Agent registry spanning v0.3 and v0.4 policies.
- **`scripts4/precision_verdict.py`** --- The pre-registered verdict on buying posterior precision.
- **`scripts4/precision_scaling.py`** --- How fast does the posterior's error fall with the sampling budget?
- **`scripts4/check_seeds.py`** --- Do two different experiments share deals they should not?
- **`fish4/learn/dataset.py`** --- Harvesting real ask positions from v0.4 self-play.
- **`scripts4/rollout_target_robust.py`** --- Is the rollout-target slope one number, or a few positions carrying it?
- **`scripts/sweep_search_params.py`** --- Tune search against EXACT ground truth instead of against other agents.
- **`scripts4/tilt_accuracy.py`** --- Does the twisted proposal estimate the same posterior, only better?
- **`tests4/test_sis_tilt.py`** --- The twisted proposal, and why it is off by default.
- **`scripts4/run_ablation_guard.py`** --- Run the ablation guard over every TunedAgent term before trusting a duel.
- **`fish4/evalx/harness.py`** --- Paired-deal evaluation harness (v0.4).
- **`scripts4/measure_calibration.py`** --- Measure the constants the whole v0.4 evaluation plan depends on.
- **`scripts4/run_typeI_simulation.py`** --- Full calibration of the sequential tests: type-I error and power.
- **`tests4/test_evalx.py`** --- Tests for the v0.4 evaluation infrastructure.
- **`fish4/evalx/sequential.py`** --- Always-valid sequential tests for paired-deal differentials.
- **`scripts/compare_variants.py`** --- Does the 54-card variant actually play differently from classic 48?
