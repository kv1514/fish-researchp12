Questions, problems and ideas

Tell us at github.com/kv1514/fish-researchp12/issues (New issue; a free GitHub account is needed). Say what you were doing, what you expected, and which phone you use. Please don't post your email address or password there.

How to play

Tap ? on the home screen for the rules. In short: two teams of three ask each other for cards, only the last two moves stay on screen, and the first team to win five sets wins.

Online tables

  • Quick Play joins a public table. If nobody else turns up within about 30 seconds, bots take the empty seats.
  • Private Table gives you a four-letter code; friends type it under Join. The host picks the time per move.
  • If a table won't load, check your connection and try again in a minute.

Reporting a player

Tap their seat at the table, or their name on the leaderboard, to open their profile, then Report cheating or Report offensive name.

Your account
