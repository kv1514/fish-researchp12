Delete your Fish account

Fish — the team card game

Deleting your account removes, immediately and permanently:

  • your sign-in account: your email address or phone number, and your Google or Apple link;
  • your profile: your name, rating, record and recent games;
  • the keys of every device you were signed in on.

Nothing is kept afterwards. Guests have no account, so there is nothing to delete for them.

In the app

Open Fish, tap your name at the top of the home screen, then Delete account.
