Privacy

Fish — the team card game

The short version

Fish has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never ask for your email, phone number, contacts, location or photos.

What we store, and for how long

  • The name you choose is shown to the other players at your table. It is stored with that table on our server and deleted when the table expires, at most 6 hours after it was last used.
  • The moves of the game (which card was asked for, who declared what) are stored with the table for the same period, so every player sees the same game.
  • On your device only: your name, sound and vibration settings, and your win/loss count are kept in the app's local storage. They never leave your device unless you join a table, and then only your name is sent.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share data with anyone.
  • We don't use advertising identifiers or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • There is no free-text chat; reactions are a fixed set of emoji.

Children

Fish does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The only thing a player types is a display name, which they can make up.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
