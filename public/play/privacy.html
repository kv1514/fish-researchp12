Privacy

Fish — the team card game

The short version

Fish has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and never asks for your contacts, location or photos. You can play as a guest with just a name, or create an account to keep a rating. You can delete your account at any time from your profile.

If you play as a guest

We keep only the name you type, with the table you play at, for at most 6 hours after the table was last used. Guests have no account and no rating, and nothing about them is kept after that.

If you create an account

  • How you sign in: an email address and password, a phone number, or your Google or Apple account — whichever you choose. Sign-in is handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase Auth, which stores your email or phone number and only a one-way hash of your password. Your password passes through our server over an encrypted connection on its way there and is never stored by us. If you use Google or Apple, we receive your name and email from them.
  • Your profile: the name you chose, your rating and best rating, your wins and losses, and a short record of your last 30 rated games. Each device you sign in on gets its own key; our server stores only a one-way hash of it, and logging out forgets that device.
  • Who sees what: other players can see your name, rating, record and recent results. Your email or phone number is never shown to anyone; your own profile shows it to you, partly hidden.
  • Deleting it: Profile → Delete account removes your profile and your sign-in account (email or phone included) from our servers immediately and permanently.

Fair play

To catch cheating in rated (Quick Play) games, the server keeps statistics about how your moves turned out compared with what the cards you could see made likely, how long you took over each move, and whether the app went to the background during your turn. These numbers are never shown to other players. If they, or reports from other players, look unusual, a person reviews them. Private tables and practice games against the bots are not analysed.

If you report another player, the report is stored with their profile, and your identity is stored only as a one-way hash, so we can count one report per person.

Tables

  • The moves of the game (which card was asked for, who declared what) are stored with the table on our server so every player sees the same game, and deleted when the table expires, at most 6 hours after it was last used.
  • On your device only: your sound and vibration settings.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share data with anyone.
  • We don't use advertising identifiers or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • There is no free-text chat; reactions are a fixed set of emoji.

Children

Fish does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The only thing a player types is a display name, which they can make up; the profile holds no real name, contact details or location.
