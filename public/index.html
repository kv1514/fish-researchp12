Fish

Six-player Literature against an engine that reads the table properly.

FishBot v0.4 tracks where every hidden card can be by counting the deals consistent with the public record — exactly, not by sampling. It also assumes you ask in suits you are deep in, because you do. You can watch both while you play.

Sit down

How it goes

  • Seats 0, 2, 4 play 1, 3, 5. You cannot see any hand but your own.
  • On your turn, ask one opponent for one specific card. You must already hold another card of its set, and not the card itself. Hit and you keep going; miss and the turn is theirs.
  • Or declare a set: name where all six cards sit within your team. Exactly right scores it. Any card with an opponent gives it away. Right team, wrong split, and it is void.
  • Nobody may bluff. So a failed ask tells the whole table that you hold another card of that set — the only signal in the game, and your opponents read it too.

Nothing is stored. A game lives in your browser tab.
