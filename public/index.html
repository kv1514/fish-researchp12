How it goes

  • Seats 0, 2, 4 play 1, 3, 5. You cannot see any hand but your own.
  • On your turn, ask one opponent for one specific card. You must already hold another card of its set, and not the card itself. Hit and you keep going; miss and the turn is theirs.
  • Or declare a set: name where all six cards sit within your team. Exactly right scores it. Get anything wrong — a card with an opponent, or the right team but the wrong split — and the whole set goes to the other team.
  • Nobody may bluff. So a failed ask tells the whole table that you hold another card of that set — the only signal in the game, and your opponents read it too.

One engine, the strongest one measured: 480-draw exact belief tracking plus depth-3 search over the posterior. Nothing is stored; a solo game lives in your browser tab.
