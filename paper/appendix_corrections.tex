\section{Corrections this revision makes to its own record}
\label{app:corrections}

Every entry below was in a previous draft of this paper and is wrong. They are
collected because a correction that appears only where the error was is a
correction most readers never see, and because the count itself is
information: a paper that reports no corrections to itself has either not been
checked or is not saying.

Nine were found by an adversarial audit of this document against the results
files, the registrations and the source, run in September 2026. The audit is
not part of the paper's method and its findings are not a result; it is
recorded here because the alternative is to present the corrected text as
though it had always read that way.

\paragraph{C1. Three claims about the other project, withdrawn.} The paper
described that engine's policy as ``frozen upstream after self-play tuning'',
quoted its author as reporting that its advantage ``does not transfer'', stated
that a ``feint'' deception feature of theirs cost $-2.2$ on replication, and
said their attribution study credited one coordinate with essentially the whole
gain of a cycle. \citet{nguyen2026sestina} gives that policy's fit objective as
minimax regret over an opponent panel and argues in \S12.1 that the advantage is
\emph{not} an artefact of self-play coordination; contains no such quotation;
lists \texttt{feint} as a member of its opponent \emph{panel}, which it beats by
$+6.07$ percentage points; and puts the coordinate's leave-one-out contribution
at $+1.69$ $[+0.80, +2.57]$ of a $+4.73$ whole while stating that the stronger
claim is not supported at its power. All four are withdrawn.

\paragraph{C2. The deployed configuration was described three ways, one false.}
The paper said the public table ``has been playing \texttt{V04\_COMBINED} all
along''. It has not: the site runs \texttt{V06\_DEPLOYED}, which differs in
carrying the endgame correction at $\texttt{endgame\_m} = 0$ --- that is, not at
all --- after this paper withdrew it. A reader could have finished the document
believing a withdrawn arm was live. Both configurations are now given verbatim
in Appendix~\ref{app:champion}.

\paragraph{C3. ``Pre-registered and replicated'' for a figure run once.} The
$+0.1220$ endgame correction was replicated against the champion at $+0.0907$
$[+0.0555, +0.1260]$; the $+0.1220$ itself, on the stronger base, is a single
$2{,}000$-pair run. The word ``replicated'' was attached to the wrong one.

\paragraph{C4. Two mixed engine pools, undisclosed.}
\path{scripts4/check_engine_provenance.py} finds two duel pools whose blocks
did not execute the same program, both quoted in this paper as primary results,
and the paper cited the checker's own $Q$ statistic as ordinary evidence that
the blocks agreed. One of the offending builds is not in the repository's
history, so those cells cannot be re-run. Disclosed in
\S\ref{sec:res-claimthreshold}.

\paragraph{C5. A table backed by its own numbers.} The four turn-price bands
are not recoverable from any results file: \path{scripts4/turn_price.py} does
not persist the per-decision values the bands are cut on, and
\path{scripts4/tempo_regime.py} carries the four prices as a constant copied
from this paper. The file that appears to back the table contains the table.
Disclosed in \S\ref{sec:tempo}.

\paragraph{C6. A selective table.} Table~\ref{tab:posterior} showed ten of the
fourteen configurations its campaign scored, and the omissions included the one
weight at which the opponent model is worse than switching it off. Two further
campaigns scored 21 more. The caption now says so.

\paragraph{C7. $\pm$ meant two things.} The symbol denoted a one-standard-error
figure in some places and a $95\%$ half-width in others, in the same document,
with no convention stated. \S\ref{sec:eval} now fixes it and the violating
sites are converted. One overclaim went with it: two turn-price bands described
as ``free, twice over'' carry $95\%$ intervals of $[-0.374, +0.288]$ and
$[-0.276, +0.284]$.

\paragraph{C8. Same model, two calibration verdicts.} The half-suit value model
was described as ``reliable in every decile'' in one section and as
miscalibrated in five of ten in another. The second is correct.

\paragraph{C9. A wrong file, a wrong percentage, and a mechanism claim beyond
its instrument.} The no-repeat result cited a results file that a later
registered run had overwritten; the repeats are $92.7\%$ of fires, not
``ninety-six percent''; and the paragraph concluded that signalling is ``a
stall that happens to communicate'', which reads one team's declaration ledger
as the whole scoreboard. The instrument counts what our three seats declare and
discards what the opposing three do. The conclusion is withdrawn.

\paragraph{C10. Two nulls that were not nulls.} The signalling protocol and the
deferred gate were both filed as ``a real reduction that buys no sets''. Both
have since been re-run at power and both buy sets: $+0.1180$ and $+0.1220$ for
signalling, $+0.0455$ $[+0.0134, +0.0776]$ for the gate. Neither clears the
$+0.15$ bar, so neither ships, and that was always the reason --- but ``buys no
sets'' was a claim the thousand-game runs could not support.

\paragraph{C11. Smaller corrections.} The introduction said three iterations
where the paper covers four. A margin-decomposition table was introduced as
$600$ games when every row is the $10{,}000$-game block. An avoided
misdeclaration was priced at $+1.7898$, the deferral gate's exchange rate,
where the signalling arms' own is $+1.2784$ $[+1.0642, +1.4926]$ and excludes
it. The volume and conversion halves of the acquisition gap were reported as
two contributions of equal size; a landed ask retains the turn, so the measured
hit rates alone predict the observed ask-count edge and the volume term is what
the conversion term implies. The conclusions covered only the first of four
development cycles. The reproduction section said v0.3's code was untouched,
after fifteen commits including the award-rule correction, and gave no
repository URL. An earlier draft asserted that a half-suit's split is
frozen once a team holds all six, which does not follow from the rule
that makes it tempting, and an interpretation of the depth-decay term
was stated behaviourally where the evidence supports only a dilution
correction.

Corrections to individual figures are recorded inline, beside the
number each one corrects, rather than gathered here: a reader holding
an earlier version finds the correction where the disagreement is.
